# Video privacy and local recording

Updated 16 November 2026. Restart Handsay to load the new controls.

## Record yourself privately

1. Open Settings → Video privacy. **Silhouette** is the default on each page load; it replaces the entire camera image with a drawing. **Hands only** draws hands and hides face, body and background. **Camera image visible** is an explicit opt-in.
2. Start your camera for recognition. The recognizer still receives the local camera image in private modes; the preview and exported video receive drawings only. Tracking loss shows a private waiting frame, never a fallback camera image.
3. Choose **Record my camera with privacy** below the camera. The recording controls share the **Use in calls** dialog, but recording does not need OBS or the native camera helper.
4. Choose the caption mode. **Sent messages** shows reviewed messages; **Live captions (accepted words only)** includes accepted or edited draft wording, which can still be wrong. Unconfirmed previews and engine messages are excluded. The output preview shows what will be recorded.
5. Click **Record video**, sign or type, click **Stop recording**, then **Download video**. Recordings are silent canvas-only WebM files, limited to two minutes or 60 MB. No microphone or screen capture is used. Download before closing/reloading Handsay.

A recording uses a dedicated canvas and retains the strongest privacy setting reached during the clip. Changing from Silhouette to Camera image visible does not reveal camera pixels in that recording. Stop and begin a new recording to intentionally record visible camera video. Preview and call output follow the current selected setting immediately.

Silhouette and Hands only remove camera pixels; they do not guarantee anonymity. Captions, introduction text and distinctive movement can reveal personal information. Inspect your downloaded file before sharing it. Files are never automatically uploaded. Native call output sends rendered frames to the localhost helper only when explicitly started.

## Record the avatar demonstration

Choose **Try a silhouette signing test → Record avatar video**. This records an introduction, three recorded isolated signs and a limits screen. Use **Stop test / recording** to end early. The clip is silent; **Download avatar video** saves it locally. The avatar's recorded coordinates feed the actual pretrained model. Reference labels do not supply predictions, and question marks visibly mark words held for review.

The included MP4 is `output/video/Handsay-avatar-demo.mp4`: 14.25 seconds, 1280 × 720, 20 fps, H.264, no audio. Its original browser download is the adjacent WebM. The clip is a software demonstration, not a full submission pitch or a test of live human signing. These separate video artifacts are not embedded in the source ZIP.

The rendered silhouette is not a validated substitute for camera input: the separate image-tracker diagnostic previously found insufficient tracked hands. The avatar demo supplies recorded landmarks directly. No physical webcam or microphone was opened to make this clip.

Model output in this recording: hello (90%, held for review), police (84%, added in demo), giraffe (88%, added in demo). Scores are model scores, not calibrated probabilities of correctness. Demo output never enters the real interview draft, room, voice or native camera output.
